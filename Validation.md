# Zi-Vo Validation Plan (Post-MVP)

Zi-Vo is a location-based engagement platform where businesses create interactive ad campaigns, and users earn real rewards by watching and engaging correctly.

**Simple model:** businesses pay for guaranteed attention; users get rewarded for it.

## Objective

Validate two things quickly:

1. Users will engage with campaigns in exchange for rewards.
2. Businesses see value and are willing to pay.

## 7-Day Plan

### Phase 1: Business Validation (Day 1-2)

#### 1) Identify target businesses

Focus on:

- Restaurants
- Gyms
- Barbers
- Local retail stores

**Goal:** build a list of at least **10 businesses**.

#### 2) Outreach script

Use this message in person, by email, or via Instagram DM:

>I’ve built a platform that helps businesses get real engagement with their ads, notifications, or announcements by rewarding users for actually paying attention.
>I’m running a small free pilot with 100 local users where people watch your content, answer a quick question to confirm engagement, and receive a reward you define (e.g. discount or free item).
>Would you be open to a quick 10–15 min demo? I’d love to show you how it works and get your feedback.



**Goal:** get **2-3 businesses** to agree.

### Phase 2: Run Pilot Campaign (Day 3-5)

#### 3) Create campaign(s)

With each participating business:

- Upload media (image or video)
- Add 1-2 simple questions
- Add a reward (for example, "50% off first meal")
- Select a small location radius

#### 4) Recruit users manually

Use:

- Friends
- WhatsApp groups
- Local community channels

Suggested message:

> Try this app - you can get [reward] if you watch and answer correctly.

**Goal:** recruit **20-50 users**.

#### 5) Observe behavior

Track:

- Do users watch fully?
- Do they answer correctly?
- Do they care about the reward?
- Do they come back and try again?

### Phase 3: Validate Value (Day 6-7)

#### 6) Ask each business one direct question

> Would you pay to run this campaign again?

#### 7) Capture decision feedback

- If **yes**: ask, "How much would you pay?"
- If **no**: ask, "What would make this valuable enough to pay for?"

### Phase 4: Evaluate Results

#### Success signals

- Users complete campaigns
- Users care about rewards
- At least one business is willing to pay

#### Failure signals

- Users skip content or guess answers
- Engagement is low
- Businesses do not see enough value

## Ground Rules

- Do **not** build new features this week
- Do **not** optimize UI
- Do **not** assume; observe real behavior
- Focus only on usage and feedback

## Weekly Outcome Target

- 1 pilot campaign completed
- 20-50 users tested
- At least 1 clear business decision: **pay / not pay**

## Core Principle

Do not build more yet.

First, prove:

- People use it
- Someone will pay for it
